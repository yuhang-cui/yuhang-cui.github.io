I have a CSV of cleaned 1-minute ES futures bars (Databento's ES.c.0 continuous contract, with columns ts_event, instrument_id, high, low; timestamps in UTC). Please write Python code that measures whether today's RTH breaks yesterday's RTH high or low. Rules:
1. Convert timestamps to US Eastern time (US/Eastern), handling daylight saving time automatically. RTH = 09:30–16:15, meaning 1-minute bars from 09:30 to 16:14 (405 bars). Exclude the overnight session.
2. Yesterday's high / low = the highest high / lowest low of all of yesterday's RTH bars. Today's high / low also uses today's RTH only.
3. "Yesterday" is the most recent stock market trading day before today, based on the NYSE calendar: skip market holidays (for example, the Tuesday after Martin Luther King Jr. Day uses the previous Friday). Build the NYSE holidays with pandas' built-in holiday rules, and add the unscheduled closures on 2012-10-29, 2012-10-30, 2018-12-05 and 2025-01-09.
4. If that "yesterday" isn't in my data (it was removed during cleaning), skip today; don't search further back. If the two days have different instrument_id values (different contracts), skip as well.
5. Upside breakout: today's RTH high is strictly greater than yesterday's high (equal doesn't count, i.e. at least 1 tick of 0.25 above). Downside breakout: today's RTH low is strictly less than yesterday's low.
6. Put each day into one of four outcomes: up only, down only, both sides, neither. Print the sample size, the share of each outcome, the share of days that broke at least one side, and a table by year.
Please add comments to every step explaining why it's done that way.
