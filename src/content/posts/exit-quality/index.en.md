> **Bottom line** (sample): defensive exits look like they lose money, but that's probably because I only use them when a trade is already going bad.

## Two measures

- **MFE** (maximum favorable excursion): while the trade was open, how far price moved in my favor at most
- **GB** (giveback): MFE minus the profit I actually exited with

## An easy trap

If I add up P&L by exit type, defensive exits will always look terrible, because I only use them when a trade already looks wrong. A fairer comparison is: **for the same trade, what would have happened without the defensive exit?**
