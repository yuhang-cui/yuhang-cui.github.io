> **What it does**: it beeps 1 minute before each bar closes, to remind me to get back to the chart and watch how the bar closes.

> <span class="takeaway">How I use it: click "Start alarms" and go do something else. When I know the next few bars won't give me a trade, I hit "Skip next bar" or "Mute 15 min".</span>

My biggest problem as a trader is drifting off while waiting for a setup: when the wait gets long, I start looking at my phone or something else, and by the time I snap back, the signal bar has already closed.

So I needed an alarm that goes off right before each 5-minute bar closes. For example, bar 1 is 09:30–09:35, so it rings at 09:34; bar 2 at 09:39, bar 3 at 09:44, and so on.

## How to use it

1. **Click "Start alarms"**: browsers only allow sound after you've clicked on the page. Nothing will ring before you click.
2. **Keep the page open**: you can switch to another tab or app, but don't close this page, and don't let the computer go to sleep.
3. **When you know the next bars won't give you a trade**:
   - "Skip next bar" (or press N on the keyboard) skips only the next alarm;
   - "Skip next 2" and "Skip next 3" work the same way;
   - "Mute 15 min / 30 min" is handy for the slow midday stretch;
   - every bar in the list has a switch on the right, so you can turn off single bars, for example if you only want an alarm before bar 18 closes.
4. **If you change something by mistake**: hit "Restore all" (or press R).

## Settings

- **Bar size**: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes; 5 minutes by default.
- **How early it rings**: 15 seconds to 2 minutes before the close; 1 minute by default.
- **RTH hours**: 09:30–16:15 US Eastern by default; you can change it to cover only the morning, for example 09:30–11:30.
- **Volume** and **system notifications**: with notifications on, a reminder also pops up in the corner of the screen.

## A few details

- **Times are US Eastern**, with daylight saving time handled automatically. I'm in China and my computer runs on Beijing time; that doesn't matter.
- **It only rings Monday through Friday**. It doesn't skip exchange holidays (like Thanksgiving) automatically, so just hit "Pause" on those days.
- **It rings on time even in a background tab**: browsers slow down timers in background tabs, so the sounds are scheduled ahead of time on the browser's audio clock instead of relying on timers.
- **Settings and skipped bars are stored only in this computer's browser** and aren't uploaded anywhere.
