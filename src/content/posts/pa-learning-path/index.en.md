After finishing Tefi's "Price Action Basics" series and Ross's Warrior Basic & Pro courses, I finally went from "totally lost, no idea where to start" to seeing some clear structure. To help newcomers avoid the detours I took, I combined my own notes with other creators' study notes into **the cheapest, lowest-cost self-study path** I could find. It goes step by step and works for complete beginners.

A note for English readers: steps 1 to 3 and Louie's channel are in Mandarin Chinese. The Brooks Trading Course is in English.

## The order to learn in

### 1. Tefi: Price Action Basics (3 hours)

The "Price Action Basics" series (价格行为基础系列) on [Tefi Reading Charts (太妃的市场朗读)](https://www.youtube.com/@tefi_readingcharts) works well as your first structured course. It gets you the simplest price action logic quickly.

### 2. Cris & Steven, 罗晟 (12.5 hours)

"Professional price action analysis and teaching videos" (专业价格行为分析和教学视频), to strengthen the basics.

### 3. Tefi: Advanced Price Action (1.5 hours)

Back to Tefi's channel for "Advanced Price Action: the required course before the summit" (价格行为进阶：登峰造极前的必修课).

### 4. Al Brooks and Louie, side by side

How to use them: say you watch Al Brooks' module on some pattern and part of it doesn't click. Go find Louie's video on the same topic and see how he explains it.

Al Brooks has both books and videos. I'd suggest **watching the videos first, then going back to the books again and again to digest them**. A fellow learner I follow, 学委, put it well: Al Brooks' course is like a buffet. Take whatever you want, as much as you can digest. The price isn't high, so please support the real thing, and thanks to Al for sharing for so many years.
