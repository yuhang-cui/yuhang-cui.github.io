I have a CSV of cleaned 1-minute ES futures bars (Databento's ES.c.0 continuous contract, with columns ts_event, instrument_id, open, high, low, close; timestamps in UTC). Please write Python code that checks whether last week's (yesterday's) close gets touched again this week (today). Rules:
1. Convert timestamps to US Eastern time (US/Eastern), handling daylight saving time automatically. Use RTH only, 09:30–16:15, meaning 1-minute bars from 09:30 to 16:14; no overnight session.
2. Each day's open = the open of the 09:30 bar, close = the close of the 16:14 bar, high / low = the highest high / lowest low of the RTH bars.
3. Weekly: a week runs Monday to Friday. This week's open = the open of the week's first trading day; last week's close = the close of last week's last trading day. Gap = this week's open − last week's close; skip gaps of exactly 0.
4. Filled: from this week's open on, any day (any bar) whose high and low straddle last week's close. For a gap up, check whether the low ≤ last week's close; for a gap down, whether the high ≥ last week's close. Also record whether it filled on the first day.
5. Gap size = |gap| ÷ the average weekly range (each week's high − low) over the last 8 weeks, in 8 buckets: 0–5%, 5–10%, 10–15%, 15–20%, 20–30%, 30–50%, 50–100%, 100% and up.
6. Skip: weeks where last week is missing, and weeks where last week's last day and this week's first day have different instrument_id values (a contract roll). If the contract rolls mid-week, ignore the days after the roll.
7. Daily: replace "week" with "day". Yesterday = the most recent stock market trading day before today, based on the NYSE calendar (pandas' built-in holiday rules plus the unscheduled closures on 2012-10-29, 2012-10-30, 2018-12-05 and 2025-01-09). If that day isn't in the data, or the two days are on different contracts, skip today. Use the last 8 days for the average range, and change "filled right away" to "filled in the first hour (09:30–10:30)".
8. For both weekly and daily, print: the number of gaps, the share filled in the same period, the share filled right away, the fill rate for gaps up and gaps down, and the number of gaps and fill rate in each bucket.
Please add comments to every step explaining why it's done that way.
