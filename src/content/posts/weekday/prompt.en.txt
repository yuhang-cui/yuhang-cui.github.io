I have a CSV of cleaned 1-minute ES futures bars (Databento's ES.c.0 continuous contract, with columns ts_event, instrument_id, open, high, low, close; timestamps in UTC). Please write Python code that checks whether ES's RTH moves and ranges differ from Monday to Friday. Rules:
1. Convert timestamps to US Eastern time (US/Eastern), handling daylight saving time automatically. Use RTH only, 09:30–16:15, meaning 1-minute bars from 09:30 to 16:14. Open = the open of the 09:30 bar, close = the close of the 16:14 bar, high / low = the highest high / lowest low of the RTH bars.
2. Compute three numbers for each day:
   - RTH change % = (close − open) ÷ open × 100; above 0 is an up day;
   - Close-to-close change % = (today's close − yesterday's close) ÷ yesterday's close × 100. Yesterday = the most recent stock market trading day before today, based on the NYSE calendar (pandas' built-in holiday rules plus the unscheduled closures on 2012-10-29, 2012-10-30, 2018-12-05 and 2025-01-09). If that day isn't in the data, or the two days have different instrument_id values, skip the close-to-close number for that day;
   - Intraday range % = (RTH high − RTH low) ÷ open × 100.
3. Weekdays follow the calendar (in a week with a Monday holiday, Tuesday is still Tuesday).
4. Group by weekday and print, for each group: the number of days, the RTH up-day share and its 95% interval (Wilson method), the close-to-close up share, the mean and median close-to-close change, and the median range.
5. Split the data into 2010–2017 and 2018–2026 and print the same table for each half, to see whether the results hold in both.
6. Then look at Fridays, month ends and quarter ends: month end = the last stock market trading day of each month (by the NYSE calendar; months where that day isn't in the data don't count), quarter end = the month end in March, June, September and December. For each, print the number of days, the RTH up-day share and the close-to-close up share with their 95% intervals, and compare with all days. Finally, list the 5 biggest close-to-close drops.
Please add comments to every step explaining why it's done that way.
